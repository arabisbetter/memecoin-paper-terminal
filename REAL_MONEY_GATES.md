# PAPER funded-account launch gates

Real funded trading is intentionally disabled until the following server-side conditions are all satisfied:

- legal_review_complete
- legal_entity_ready
- kyc_provider_configured
- aml_sanctions_controls_ready
- jurisdiction_allowlist_ready
- custody_security_review_complete
- turnkey_signing_enabled
- treasury_capital_available
- real_funded_activation
- real_payouts_enabled

An evaluation pass is not sufficient to enable real money.
